These terms apply when you use AgricDrone Hub, a marketplace that connects farm owners with drone pilots for agricultural mapping, surveying, monitoring and inspection. By creating an account you agree to them. Please also read our Privacy policy.

What AgricDrone Hub does

  • Farm owners post jobs, verified pilots send bids, and the farm owner chooses a pilot.
  • AgricDrone Hub is not a party to the agreement between a farm owner and a pilot, does not fly drones, and does not handle payments. Prices in jobs and bids are what the two of you agree; payment is arranged between you.
  • Our verification of a pilot is an internal check of the documents they upload. It is not approval by the South African Civil Aviation Authority (SACAA) and does not mean any particular flight is lawful.

Your account

  • Give true, current details and keep them up to date in Profile.
  • Keep your password private. You are responsible for what happens on your account.
  • One account per person. Admin accounts can't be signed up for.

If you are a pilot

  • You may bid only while you hold a valid SACAA Remote Pilot Licence and operate under a valid Remote Operating Certificate (your own or your employer's), and while any aircraft you use is approved and registered as the law requires.
  • Upload true, current documents. Tell us at once if a licence or certificate is suspended, withdrawn or expires; you can't bid while your verified documents have expired.
  • Fly every job within the law, including Part 101 of the Civil Aviation Regulations, the limits of your licence and certificate, airspace rules and the landowner's permission.
  • Agricultural spraying is on compliance hold on AgricDrone Hub: spraying jobs can't be bid on or arranged through the platform until our legal review is complete.

If you are a farm owner

  • Describe each job truthfully, including where the farm is and what is needed.
  • Only post work you are entitled to arrange on that land, and don't ask a pilot to fly in a way that breaks aviation or other laws.
  • Confirm a job is complete only once the work has been delivered, and rate pilots honestly.

Not allowed

  • Fraud, fake or misleading job posts, bids or reviews.
  • Arranging unsafe or unlawful flying, or anything that bypasses licences, certificates, airspace limits or other aviation rules.
  • Harassing or abusing other users.
  • Uploading someone else's documents, or trying to access other people's accounts or data.

Reports, removal and suspension

Anyone can report a job post or review. Our admins may dismiss a report, remove a job post (the owner is told why), hide a review or suspend an account. A suspended account can't sign in. To appeal, email tiyanithebest@gmail.com.

Ratings and reviews

A farm owner can rate the pilot once for each completed job. Reviews must be honest and about the work done. A pilot can report a review they believe is unfair, and an admin decides whether it stays visible.

Responsibility

Pilots are responsible for how they fly and farm owners for what they ask for. To the extent the law allows, AgricDrone Hub is not responsible for the work, conduct or payments between farm owners and pilots, or for losses caused by them. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have under South African law, including the Consumer Protection Act, that can't be limited.
