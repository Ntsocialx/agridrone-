Type ·   difficulty · suggested team size
Supporting   · Medium-Hard · 3 students (or one student on the core slice)
Purpose
A   marketplace connecting certified drone operators with farms that need   surveying, mapping, monitoring and inspection work.
Why it   exists
Drone   training often ends at licensing, leaving graduates with no route to work.   The hub closes the loop between training and earning, within legal limits.
Primary   users
Operators,   farm owners, administrators.
Web   features (minimum)
•   Operator profile with licence and certificate fields.
•   Admin verification of licence status; unverified operators cannot accept   jobs.
•   Farm posts a job (service type, location, date).
•   Operators bid or accept; farm confirms.
•   Restricted service types (for example spraying) display a legal warning.
Mobile /   PWA features
•   Installable PWA; job alerts; geolocation for job sites.
•   Photo upload of licence documents.
Provides
Employment   outcomes to the impact product.
Consumes
Passport   credentials (certified operator); identity.
Data   entities
operators,   licences, farms, jobs, bids, verifications.
Golden   journey (success test)
Operator   uploads licence, admin verifies, farm posts a job, operator accepts, farm   confirms.
Stretch goal
Rating   after job completion.
Cautions
All   drone operation in South Africa is regulated by the civil aviation authority.   The platform must verify licensing status and must not facilitate unlawful   operation; include this rule in AGENTS.md.
